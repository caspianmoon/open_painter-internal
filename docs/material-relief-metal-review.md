# Render review: materials 1–16

Reviewed each new render individually against its previous render. Both recorded contexts use the curved sample, 2048×2048 authored textures, 512×512 renders and 32 Cycles samples. Previous images: `.blender-test/material-corrections/final-2k/`; reviewed images: `.blender-test/material-relief/final/`. Rows 4, 8, 9, 10 and 11 include the separately rendered follow-up in `.blender-test/material-relief/tuned/`, which supersedes those first-pass observations.

The clearest improvements are visible brushing/casting relief and the irregular coating loss in Heavy Chipped Paint and Rusting Painted Steel. The follow-up balances Scratched Aluminum's initially excessive bright scoring and restores readable scuffs on Worn Painted Metal. Military Painted Steel remains lightly used at this viewing size. Several previously detailed metals retain a similar overall appearance.

| # | Material | Actual observation in the new render | Comparison / remaining concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clean Brushed Steel | Fine lateral tooling visibly breaks the three bright highlights. The dark lower hemisphere remains quiet. | Clear relief improvement over the previously almost smooth reflections. Direction wraps into rings on this sphere; no repeating short scratch marks. |
| 2 | Worn Brushed Steel | Denser irregular fine scoring and more broken highlights than the clean steel. Lower groove wear remains visible. | Clearly rougher than the previous smooth version; convincingly distinct from Clean Brushed Steel in wear strength. |
